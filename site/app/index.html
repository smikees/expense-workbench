    A little context goes a long way

    Incoming (+) means money received; outgoing (−) means money paid out. An incoming payment may be a refund, income or a repayment—we keep its purpose under review until confirmed. Use the note icon next to any payment to provide context for a later review. Saving a note does not resolve the item or change its category. New or edited notes appear here until reviewed. You can also open Firefly to edit a category, then refresh this dashboard.

    Know what’s in your numbers

    Bank syncing runs separately from the dashboard. Refresh reloads the saved ledger and latest sync status.

    Automatic bank sync

    Scheduled runs require the configured worker to be available. Bank access is read-only. New booked transactions are checked against saved imports; internal transfers stay outside spending. Consent renewal and ambiguous matches need your attention. Unsupported accounts use statement imports.

    Coverage by account

    Money lent
